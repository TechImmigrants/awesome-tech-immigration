## Resume & CV

### General Rules

1. **No personal info** — no age, gender, photo (unless required by country), marital status, or full home address
2. **Contact info only** — phone number, email, LinkedIn, GitHub/portfolio
3. **Tailor every CV** — match keywords from the job description. ATS systems filter before a human reads
4. **Quantify impact** — "Reduced API latency by 40%" beats "Improved performance"
5. **One page** for < 10 years experience. Two pages max for senior roles
6. **PDF format** — unless the application specifically asks for .docx

## Country Guides

### 🇩🇪 Germany

- **Visa:** EU Blue Card (most common for tech). Requires a job offer with minimum salary
- **CV norms:** Photo is common (but not legally required). Lebenslauf format. 1-2 pages
- **Job market:** Berlin, Munich, Hamburg, Frankfurt. Strong demand for backend, ML, data engineering
- **Language:** English is enough for most tech roles. German helps for daily life and non-tech roles
- **Resources:**
  - [Make it in Germany (Official)](https://www.make-it-in-germany.com/en/)
  - [Germany Visa Guide](https://www.germany-visa.org/)
  - [Blue Card EU Guide](https://www.bluecard-eu.de/eu-blue-card-germany/)

### 🇫🇮 Finland

- **Visa:** Specialist Visa (D visa), fast-track processing. Finland Talent Boost program
- **CV norms:** International format. No photo needed. 1-2 pages
- **Job market:** Helsinki metro area dominates. Gaming (Supercell, Rovio), telecom (Nokia), health-tech
- **Language:** English is widely spoken in tech. Finnish is hard but valued for career growth
- **Resources:**
  - [Work in Finland (Official)](https://www.workinfinland.com/)
  - [Helsinki Business Hub — Talent](https://www.helsinkibusinesshub.fi/talent/)
  - [InfoFinland.fi](https://www.infofinland.fi/en)

### 🇳🇱 Netherlands

- **Visa:** Kennismigrant (Highly Skilled Migrant) visa. 30% ruling = tax benefit for 5 years
- **CV norms:** International format. 1-2 pages. No photo
- **Job market:** Amsterdam, Eindhoven, Rotterdam. Strong in fintech, logistics tech, semiconductors (ASML)
- **Resources:**
  - [IND — Highly Skilled Migrant](https://ind.nl/en/residence-permits/work/highly-skilled-migrant)
  - [Dutch 30% Ruling Explained](https://www.iamsterdam.com/en/living/take-care-of-official-matters/highly-skilled-migrants/thirty-percent-ruling)

### 🇸🇪 Sweden

- **Visa:** Work permit through employer. Processing 1-4 months
- **CV norms:** International format. No photo. Lagom — not too flashy, not too modest
- **Job market:** Stockholm (major tech hub), Gothenburg. Spotify, Klarna, King, Ericsson
- **Resources:**
  - [Work in Sweden (Official)](https://www.sweden.se/work-business/work-in-sweden)
  - [Swedish Migration Agency](https://www.migrationsverket.se/English/Private-individuals/Working-in-Sweden.html)

### 🇨🇦 Canada

- **Visa:** Express Entry (Federal Skilled Worker, CEC, PNP). Points-based system
- **CV norms:** No photo. No personal info. 2 pages is standard. "Resume" not "CV"
- **Job market:** Toronto, Vancouver, Montreal, Ottawa, Waterloo. AI/ML hub (Montreal, Toronto)
- **Resources:**
  - [Express Entry (Official)](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry.html)
  - [NOC Code Finder](https://www.canada.ca/en/immigration-refugees-citizenship/services/immigrate-canada/express-entry/eligibility/find-national-occupation-code.html)
  - [WES Credential Evaluation](https://www.wes.org/ca/)

### 🇦🇹 Austria

- **Visa:** Red-White-Red Card for skilled workers. Points-based
- **CV norms:** Photo is common. Europass format accepted. German helps
- **Job market:** Vienna. Growing startup scene
- **Resources:**
  - [Migration.gv.at (Official)](https://www.migration.gv.at/en/)

### 🇦🇪 UAE

- **Visa:** Employment visa through employer. Golden Visa for high earners/specialists
- **CV norms:** Photo is common. Personal details expected. 2-3 pages
- **Job market:** Dubai, Abu Dhabi. Fintech, e-commerce, government digitization
- **Resources:**
  - [MOHRE (Official)](https://www.mohre.gov.ae/en/home.aspx)

## Visa Types for Tech Workers

| Visa Type | Country | Key Requirement |
|-----------|---------|----------------|
| EU Blue Card | Germany, most EU | Job offer + min salary |
| Specialist Visa | Finland | Job offer in specialist role |
| Kennismigrant | Netherlands | Job offer + min salary |
| Tier 2 (Skilled Worker) | UK | Job offer + sponsor license |
| H-1B | USA | Employer sponsorship + lottery |
| Express Entry | Canada | Points-based (age, education, experience, language) |
| Global Talent Visa | UK | Exceptional talent in tech/science |
| O-1 | USA | Extraordinary ability |
| Startup Visa | Canada, UK, NL | Viable startup + endorsement |

## Language Certification

### English
- [IELTS](https://www.ielts.org/) — required for Canada, UK, Australia
- [TOEFL](https://www.ets.org/toefl) — widely accepted, especially in North America
- [Duolingo English Test](https://englishtest.duolingo.com/) — cheaper, accepted by some programs

### German
- [Goethe-Institut](https://www.goethe.de/en/spr/kup/prf.html) — the gold standard
- [TestDaF](https://www.testdaf.de/en/) — required for German university admission
- [telc](https://www.telc.net/en/) — accepted for work/residency

### French
- [TEF Canada](https://www.lefrancaisdesaffaires.fr/en/tests-diplomas/tef-canada/) — for Canadian immigration
- [DELF/DALF](https://www.france-education-international.fr/en/delf-dalf) — general French proficiency

### Dutch
- [Inburgering Exam](https://inburgeren.nl/en/) — required for permanent residency in Netherlands

## منابع فارسی

### نکات عمومی رزومه کاری

1. رزومه نباید حاوی اطلاعات شخصی نظیر سن، جنسیت، آدرس دقیق محل سکونت، کد پستی و ... باشد
2. تنها اطلاعات شخصی در رزومه باید شماره تماس و ایمیل باشد
3. قراردادن لینک پروفایل گیتهاب، استک اورفلو و لینکداین می‌تواند مفید باشد
4. هر رزومه را برای هر شغل سفارشی کنید — کلمات کلیدی آگهی را استفاده کنید
